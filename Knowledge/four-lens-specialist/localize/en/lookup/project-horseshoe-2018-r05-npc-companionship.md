# Designing Feelings of Companionship with NPCs — Lookup Summary

## Five Companion Factors
Companionship can be analyzed through personhood, bonding, investment, value, and stakes. Personhood asks whether the NPC appears autonomous and internally alive; bonding comes from shared experience and trust; investment captures player resources; value makes interaction useful; stakes make loss or change consequential. A companion need not maximize every factor.
→ see learned/project-horseshoe-2018-r05-npc-companionship.md § Five Companion Factors

## Strong Predictors and Failure Modes
The report's survey found personhood highly predictive, value and stakes strong, bonding moderate, and investment weak. Best-in-class strength in a few dimensions can outperform shallow coverage of all five. Bugs, repetitive dialogue, direct puppet-like control, forgotten choices, disposability, and liability behavior break companionship.
→ see learned/project-horseshoe-2018-r05-npc-companionship.md § Strong Predictors and Failure Modes

## Designing Attachment
Give companions distinctive motivations, signs of a life beyond the player, natural world interaction, useful synergy, memorable shared moments, and credible consequences. Balance autonomy with reliability so personhood does not become frustration. Test attachment soon after play and separate liking, utility, importance, and companionship in research questions.
→ see learned/project-horseshoe-2018-r05-npc-companionship.md § Designing Attachment

## Key Takeaways for Companion Design
- Select two or three factors to make exceptional. → learned/project-horseshoe-2018-r05-npc-companionship.md § Five Companion Factors
- Prioritize personhood, useful value, and credible stakes. → learned/project-horseshoe-2018-r05-npc-companionship.md § Strong Predictors and Failure Modes
- Treat repetitive or incompetent behavior as relationship damage. → learned/project-horseshoe-2018-r05-npc-companionship.md § Strong Predictors and Failure Modes
- Measure companionship separately from simple liking. → learned/project-horseshoe-2018-r05-npc-companionship.md § Designing Attachment
