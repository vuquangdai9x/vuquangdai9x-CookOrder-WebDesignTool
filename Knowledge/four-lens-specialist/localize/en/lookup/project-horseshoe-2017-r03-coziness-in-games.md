# Coziness in Games — Lookup Summary

## Safety Abundance and Softness
Coziness is the fantasy of safety, abundance, and softness. Safety removes imminent loss and makes vulnerability and participation voluntary. Abundance satisfies pressing needs so attention can move to belonging, mastery, reflection, and care. Softness signals a gentle, intimate, sincere, low-arousal environment without requiring disengagement.
→ see learned/project-horseshoe-2017-r03-coziness-in-games.md § Safety Abundance and Softness

## Protecting the Cozy State
Extrinsic pressure, danger, compulsory maintenance, intrusive notifications, intense stimulus, deception, non-consensual presence, and confinement redirect attention toward urgent lower-order needs. Contrast can strengthen coziness when the threat remains safely outside a trusted boundary. A campfire in a storm works because the shelter is legible and reliable; a leaking roof breaks the promise.
→ see learned/project-horseshoe-2017-r03-coziness-in-games.md § Protecting the Cozy State

## Cozy Pacing and Practice
Cozy spaces can be whole games or moments of respite inside stressful games. They give players control of pacing and room to practice higher-order needs such as connection, nurturing, and self-expression. Consent is essential: forced intimacy, dishonest stakes, or a culture that suppresses dissent may look pleasant while lacking psychological safety.
→ see learned/project-horseshoe-2017-r03-coziness-in-games.md § Cozy Pacing and Practice
